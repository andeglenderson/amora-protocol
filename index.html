Mass-market agent protocols — built by payment giants and enterprise platforms — are designed for price comparison, volume, and margin compression. Three gates. Total control.

PARK

Entry is free. Every asset submitted to the registry is reviewed using AI vision authentication before indexing. Quality is the only entry requirement. Once verified, your asset receives a machine-readable schema — indexed for discovery by AI agents and autonomous buying systems worldwide, invisible to the public web.

VERIFY

Every asset carries a live reputation score built from real transactions. New assets are fee-exempt for their first five verified transactions. Maintain your score above benchmark and parking remains free permanently. Drop below and a small reinstatement fee applies. The registry has memory. Standards are enforced, not assumed.

UNLOCK

When qualified intent arrives, the gate opens on your terms. You set the unlock price. You receive a vetted, translated intent packet containing everything you need to close. High-value cross-border transactions are settled via recommended escrow rails — no banks, no intermediaries, instant confirmation for both parties.
